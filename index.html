How the index works, and what it cannot prove＋

Aᵢ = 100 × Oᵢ × maxg [ U(g) × Rᵢ(g) × B(g) ]

Eight common local candidate offsets are screened for bounds, a 2.5-second pass horizon, receiver arrival and positional offside at release. Region preference, readiness and lane score all refer to the same selected point. A feasible controller estimates receiver readiness; an optimistic arrival-time lower bound screens defensive threats. Neither is a validated player model.

These are authored synthetic scenes, not real tracking data. The model covers direct receiving options, not defender attraction, future goals or causal player contribution. Fixed surroundings and a limited candidate grid can change the conclusion. A zero score is not proof that every real pass is impossible.
