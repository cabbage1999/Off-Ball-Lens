What this lens measures＋

Space, with a clear meaning

AS is effective receiving area in m². DAS weights that area by the author's danger model. We read both from accessible-space 2.1.0, using all 22 players, the ball, possession and attack direction.

A view of opportunity

This is direct receiving opportunity, not a goal or pass-success probability, and not causal credit for a run. Individual areas can overlap. A decoy can help a teammate without increasing its own DAS.

Recorded and reproducible

Three independently authored synthetic attacks. Tracking is sampled at 10 Hz; author analysis is precomputed at 5 Hz. Animation interpolates positions. Heatmap colour has a fixed scale. Offside is a positional approximation.
